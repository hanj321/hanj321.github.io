## PhD students

### Byungkyu Choi

Byungkyu is a PhD student in Industrial, Manufacturing, and Systems Engineering at Texas Tech University. He received his B.S. and M.S. degrees in Industrial and Management Engineering from Incheon National University.

His research interests lie in human factors and ergonomics, with a focus on understanding and improving human performance in human-centered systems. He is particularly interested in how human movement, neuromuscular demands, and adaptation influence performance during interactions with emerging technologies.
